01 — The problem
0%

Central Water Commission · 1990 → 2020

The water is moving faster than the maps.

Rapid urbanisation sealed the ground that used to absorb monsoon runoff. Climate change made that runoff heavier and less predictable. Together they drove flood losses up more than fourteenfold in three decades.

Yet risk assessment in India still runs on historical averages at state resolution — too coarse to tell a planner which ward floods, or an underwriter which street to price differently.

FloodGuard closes that gap: satellite observation, climate models and hydrological simulation, resolved to the district and taluka.
