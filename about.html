Who we are

A flood-risk company, built in Hyderabad, for India.

Flood is India's most frequent natural hazard — yet most decisions about it still rest on fragmented, backward-looking data. FloodGuard exists to change that: one consistent flood record, modelled into intelligence that insurers, lenders, planners and governments can act on.
