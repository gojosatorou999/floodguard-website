Applications

Five applications for infrastructure

01Site screening

Assess flood risk before selecting or developing a site.

02Asset risk

Understand flood exposure for existing infrastructure.

03Infrastructure corridors

Assess flood risk along roads, railways, pipelines and other corridors.

04Project planning

Incorporate flood intelligence into infrastructure planning.

05Climate resilience

Understand how flood risk could evolve over time.

Who it's for

Assets that will face the water for decades.

Who

Developers, engineers, utilities and investors

Teams siting, designing or financing roads, rail, power, water and real estate.

The decision

Is this site — or this route — safe from flooding?

Today, and over the decades the asset is expected to last.

What you get

Site and corridor screening

  • Flood risk along a route or at a site
  • Assets in high-risk segments
  • Projected change to 2050 and 2100
